Atelier Inquiries

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

Everything you need to know about our metals, sizing, and dispatches.

All DRAYEN jewelry is constructed from 316L surgical stainless steel or heavy solid pewter casts with hand-applied antique oxidation finishes. Our premium runs utilize genuine .925 sterling silver hallmarked components. They are 100% nickel-free and hypoallergenic.
Never. Our 316L stainless steel does not oxidize, rust, or leave green discoloration on skin, even with direct exposure to sweat, sea water, or daily showers. The antiqued dark highlights are permanently sealed.
Most of our statement pendants come paired with an adjustable 20-inch to 24-inch (50cm – 60cm) heavy wheat or curb chain, designed to sit naturally on the chest over crewnecks and hoodies. Precise specs are specified on each product page.
Simply rinse with lukewarm water and mild soap, then wipe dry with a microfiber cloth. Avoid harsh chemical dips or ultrasonic jewelry cleaners, as these can strip the intended vintage patina in the recessed engravings.
Metro orders are dispatched within 24 hours and typically arrive within 2 to 4 business days. Regional destinations take 4 to 6 business days. Full tracking details are emailed and WhatsApp messaged instantly upon dispatch.
We offer a hassle-free 7-day replacement or store credit guarantee if your piece arrives damaged or does not match your expectations. Items must remain unworn in original matte packaging.
